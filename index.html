Introduction

A safer way into home ownership.

HomeChance SA is a mobile app for the South African property market. Sellers list a home, buyers enter a ticket draw for it, and a random draw picks the winner.

The difference is trust. Every listing is identity-checked, ownership-checked, risk-assessed by AI and approved by a person before it goes live, and the whole journey is tracked through to the legal transfer.

Property is slow, costly, and increasingly faked.

South Africa’s property market faces two linked challenges: inefficiency and fraud.

Slow and expensive

Transactions involve high commission fees and paperwork scattered between estate agents, banks, conveyancers and the Deeds Office.

Fraud is rising

Criminals use AI-generated fake listings, forged title deeds, identity theft and impersonation of real agencies.

Nothing joins it up

Existing platforms mostly advertise properties. None combine verification, secure documents and transaction tracking in one place.

HomeChance SA brings verification, document management and transaction tracking into one mobile app.

Buy a ticket. Win a home.

Participants buy tickets for a verified house and the app runs a random draw to pick the winner. Sellers avoid long listing periods; the winner gets a home at a lower price.

List

The seller submits the property with identity and ownership documents.

Verify

An AI risk check and address lookup run, then an administrator approves or rejects.

Enter

Verified participants buy tickets for the draw.

Draw

When the draw closes, the system randomly selects a winner, who is verified again.

Transfer

A conveyancing attorney handles clearances and Deeds Office registration.

Worked example: Sarah lists her three-bedroom Johannesburg house. Once her identity and ownership are verified, it goes live. Thabo buys entries, is drawn as the winner and passes a final check. After the legal transfer, he is the owner and Sarah receives the agreed proceeds under the platform’s terms.

Implementation details

How we built it

The parts of the build we are proudest of, and how each one works.

Authentication and roles

Email and Google sign-in through Supabase Auth. The app routes users by the role stored in the database, never by editable user metadata. Three wrong passwords lock sign-in for one minute.

Four-step listing submission

A validated form with photo grid and document upload. If any save fails, uploaded files and the half-created listing are rolled back.

AI fraud review

The verify-property function adds an address lookup, duplicate detection and a Groq risk assessment. Hard rules stop the AI lowering a score below the facts.

Document security

Files live in private buckets and open only through signed links that expire after 60 seconds. Verified documents cannot be deleted.

Database protection

Row Level Security limits rows to owners and administrators. Profiles can change only a fixed set of fields, so role and verification cannot be edited from the app.

Support and notifications

Help Centre and Contact Support send an email to the team and a confirmation to the user automatically.
